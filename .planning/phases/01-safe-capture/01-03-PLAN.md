## Phase Goal

**As a** single developer who captures learnings from the terminal on Windows, **I want to** install `til` once and record a one-line learning from any shell into today's local-date Markdown file, **so that** every entry is kept exactly as typed — Korean and emoji included — across repeated runs, concurrent runs, and failed writes.
